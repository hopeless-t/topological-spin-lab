# References and source registry

This repository treats citations as part of the scientific boundary, not as decoration.

## Citation policy

1. Prefer original research papers for scientific claims.
2. Use reviews for orientation, not as substitutes for known primary sources.
3. Keep institutional press releases as provenance for how the project was triggered, while citing the paper itself for scientific results.
4. A citation means that a source informed a statement or experiment design. It does not mean this repository reproduces that material, measurement, calculation, or conclusion.
5. Material-specific parameters must carry source provenance before they may be described as material-informed.
6. Do not copy figures, tables, or datasets from papers unless license and attribution requirements have been checked explicitly. Linking to the original source is preferred.
7. State coordinate, surface-normal, basis, and sign conventions explicitly when sources use different conventions.

The relationships among these sources are documented in LITERATURE_MAP.md.

## Project trigger and direct NdBi lineage

### [P-NDBI-2026] Fukushima et al.

Yuto Fukushima, Hiroyuki S. Suzuki, Masayuki Ochi, Takuma Nishida, Toshiya Ikenobe, Hiraku Saito, Yuto Kinoshita, Kaishu Kawaguchi, Hiroaki Tanaka, Ayumi Harasawa, Takushi Iimori, Koichiro Yaji, Masashi Tokunaga, Taro Nakajima, Kiyohisa Tanaka, Takahiro Morimoto, Ryo Mori, and Takeshi Kondo.

"Direct observation of a spin-polarized surface Dirac gap in the antiferromagnetic topological insulator NdBi."
Nature Communications (2026).

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77571-4

Role in this repository: immediate scientific trigger. The paper reports a spin-polarized topological surface Dirac cone in NdBi with a 17 ± 2 meV gap and links gap opening to surface magnetic order. It is a future comparison target, not the source of a material-specific EXP-001 parameter set.

### [I-ISSP-2026] Institute for Solid State Physics, The University of Tokyo

"反強磁性体の表面に、磁石の性質を帯びた奇妙な電子が出現 ― スピンの向きが100％揃った電流の実現へ ―", 2026-09-17.

https://www.issp.u-tokyo.ac.jp/maincontents/news2.html?pid=32233

Role in this repository: institutional source through which this project was initiated. Scientific claims are traced back to [P-NDBI-2026].

### [P-NDBI-2023] Honma et al.

A. Honma, D. Takane, S. Souma, et al.

"Antiferromagnetic topological insulator with selectively gapped Dirac cones."
Nature Communications 14, 7396 (2023).

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-42782-6

Role in this repository: direct NdBi predecessor. The work established domain-dependent gapped and gapless surface Dirac states and tied their distinction to combined antiferromagnetic symmetry.

## Surface Dirac state and spin-momentum-locking lineage

### [P-TI-2007] Fu and Kane

Liang Fu and C. L. Kane.
"Topological insulators with inversion symmetry."
Physical Review B 76, 045302 (2007).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.76.045302

Role: foundational primary source for three-dimensional time-reversal-invariant topological-insulator classification and protected surface states.

### [P-BI2SE3-2009] Zhang et al.

Haijun Zhang, Chao-Xing Liu, Xiao-Liang Qi, Xi Dai, Zhong Fang, and Shou-Cheng Zhang.
"Topological insulators in Bi2Se3, Bi2Te3 and Sb2Te3 with a single Dirac cone on the surface."
Nature Physics 5, 438–442 (2009).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys1270

Role: bridge from topological classification to a concrete low-energy Dirac description of experimentally relevant 3D topological-insulator surfaces.

### [P-SPIN-2009] Hsieh et al.

D. Hsieh, Y. Xia, D. Qian, et al.
"A tunable topological insulator in the spin helical Dirac transport regime."
Nature 460, 1101–1105 (2009).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08234

Role: primary experimental precedent for a spin-momentum-locked surface Dirac cone; motivates EXP-001 spin observables.

### [P-MODEL-2010] Liu et al.

Chao-Xing Liu, Xiao-Liang Qi, Haijun Zhang, Xi Dai, Zhong Fang, and Shou-Cheng Zhang.
"Model Hamiltonian for topological insulators."
Physical Review B 82, 045122 (2010).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.82.045122

Role: primary model-Hamiltonian reference for effective low-energy surface-state descriptions. EXP-001 deliberately uses a minimal generic surface Dirac Hamiltonian and fixes its own +z surface-normal and helicity convention.

## Topological response and magnetic-gap lineage

### [P-TFT-2008] Qi, Hughes, and Zhang

Xiao-Liang Qi, Taylor L. Hughes, and Shou-Cheng Zhang.
"Topological field theory of time-reversal invariant insulators."
Physical Review B 78, 195424 (2008).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.78.195424

Role: primary theoretical source connecting 3D topological insulators to topological response theory.

### [P-AXION-2009] Essin, Moore, and Vanderbilt

Andrew M. Essin, Joel E. Moore, and David Vanderbilt.
"Magnetoelectric Polarizability and Axion Electrodynamics in Crystalline Insulators."
Physical Review Letters 102, 146805 (2009).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.102.146805

Role: primary source for magnetoelectric/axion response language used by later magnetic and antiferromagnetic topological-insulator work.

### [P-QAH-2010] Yu et al.

Rui Yu, Wei Zhang, Hai-Jun Zhang, Shou-Cheng Zhang, Xi Dai, and Zhong Fang.
"Quantized anomalous Hall effect in magnetic topological insulators."
Science 329, 61–64 (2010).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1187485

Role: theory lineage for why opening a magnetic gap in a topological surface state can be physically consequential.

### [P-QAH-2013] Chang et al.

Cui-Zu Chang, Jinsong Zhang, Xiao Feng, et al.
"Experimental observation of the quantum anomalous Hall effect in a magnetic topological insulator."
Science 340, 167–170 (2013).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1234414

Role: experimental landmark connecting magnetic topological-insulator theory to quantized transport.

## Antiferromagnetic topological-insulator lineage

### [P-AFM-2010] Mong, Essin, and Moore

Roger S. K. Mong, Andrew M. Essin, and Joel E. Moore.
"Antiferromagnetic topological insulators."
Physical Review B 81, 245209 (2010).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.81.245209

Role: foundational primary source for AFM topological insulators protected by combined time reversal and lattice translation. It also predicts gapped symmetry-breaking surfaces and chiral one-dimensional modes at certain step edges.

### [P-MBT-AXION-2019] Zhang et al.

Dongqin Zhang, Minji Shi, Tongshuai Zhu, Dingyu Xing, Haijun Zhang, and Jing Wang.
"Topological Axion States in the Magnetic Insulator MnBi2Te4 with the Quantized Magnetoelectric Effect."
Physical Review Letters 122, 206401 (2019).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.206401

Role: material-specific theoretical bridge from AFM-TI symmetry to an intrinsic magnetic-material platform.

### [P-MBT-2019] Otrokov et al.

M. M. Otrokov, I. I. Klimovskikh, H. Bentmann, et al.
"Prediction and observation of an antiferromagnetic topological insulator."
Nature 576, 416–422 (2019).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1840-9

Role: major material realization of the intrinsic AFM-TI concept in MnBi2Te4.

### [P-MBT-GAPLESS-2019] Hao et al.

Y.-J. Hao, P. Liu, Y. Feng, et al.
"Gapless Surface Dirac Cone in Antiferromagnetic Topological Insulator MnBi2Te4."
Physical Review X 9, 041038 (2019).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.9.041038

Role: primary experimental example of the theory/experiment tension around magnetic surface gaps. That tension is part of the context for the later NdBi results.

## Repository convention note

The sign and apparent helicity of an effective surface Hamiltonian depend on surface-normal, coordinate, basis, and Pauli-matrix conventions. EXP-001 fixes:

~~~text
surface normal = +z
H(k) = alpha * (kx sigma_y - ky sigma_x)
upper-band helicity = +1
~~~

A different convention in a cited paper is not treated as a disagreement unless the physical observables differ.
