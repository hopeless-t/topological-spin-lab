# EXP-002 — Magnetic mass / Dirac gap

## Status

**Implemented; scientific contract active.**

EXP-002 is the first controlled perturbation of the canonical EXP-001 massless
surface Dirac reference.

## Hamiltonian

With the repository convention `surface normal = +z`,

$$
H_m(\mathbf{k})
=
\alpha(k_x\sigma_y-k_y\sigma_x)
+
m\sigma_z.
$$

The canonical parameters are:

~~~text
alpha = 1.0 eV·Å
m     = +0.020 eV
~~~

The mass is deliberately a **generic reference value**. It is not fitted to
NdBi or any other material.

## Analytic spectrum

$$
E_\pm
=
\pm\sqrt{\alpha^2(k_x^2+k_y^2)+m^2}.
$$

At `k = 0` the direct gap is

$$
\Delta = 2|m|.
$$

For the canonical mass, `Delta = 0.040 eV`.

## Analytic spin

Define

$$
\mathbf{d}
=
(-\alpha k_y,\alpha k_x,m).
$$

For the upper band,

$$
\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rangle_+
=
\frac{\mathbf{d}}{|\mathbf{d}|},
$$

and the lower-band spin is the negative of this vector.

Consequences:

- spin remains perpendicular to in-plane momentum;
- a nonzero `m` tilts spin out of the surface plane;
- at the Dirac-point momentum, the upper-band spin points along
  `sign(m) z`;
- ring helicity is reduced from unit magnitude to
  `alpha k / sqrt((alpha k)^2 + m^2)`.

## Time reversal

For spin-1/2 time reversal, the massless reference is invariant. The
`m sigma_z` term changes sign under time reversal.

EXP-002 therefore checks two separate statements:

1. comparing the same mass on both sides produces the expected nonzero
   time-reversal residual of `2|m|`;
2. flipping the mass restores the relation
   `T H_m(k) T^-1 = H_-m(-k)`.

The second relation is an internal model consistency check. It does not claim
that a real material can be converted between magnetic domains without other
changes.

## Canonical acceptance checks

EXP-002 must verify:

- Hamiltonian Hermiticity;
- agreement with the independent analytic spectrum;
- direct-gap agreement with `2|m|`;
- spectral pairing `E_lower + E_upper = 0` in this traceless reference model;
- normalized spin expectation;
- in-plane spin-momentum orthogonality;
- full spin-vector agreement with the analytic solution;
- reduced-helicity agreement with the analytic solution;
- center-spin agreement with the sign of the mass;
- expected time-reversal-breaking magnitude;
- time-reversal covariance under `m -> -m`.

## Scientific boundary

EXP-002 may claim only that a generic two-band surface Dirac model with a
uniform `sigma_z` mass term reproduces its known analytic gap, spin tilt, and
time-reversal properties.

EXP-002 does **not** establish:

- an NdBi material model;
- a measured magnetic exchange field;
- an antiferromagnetic topological-insulator classification;
- a Chern number;
- a quantum anomalous Hall state;
- an axion-insulator state;
- an edge state;
- transport quantization.

Those require additional structure and experiments.
